# 2024/25 home shirt marks — Panathinaikos

These are used for the illustrated `cof-k-56` archive drawing. The drawing is an approximation, not a photo or claim of pixel-exact reproduction.

- `stoiximan-2024.svg`: transparent, dark-green-and-white treatment of the path and polygon shapes from [Stoiximan (logo).svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stoiximan_(logo).svg), attributed there to Stoiximan; Commons classifies the source as PD-textlogo and notes trademark restrictions. Blue background removed, fill, outline and viewBox changed. The source is a 2024 corporate mark, and the on-shirt treatment is based on the reference below.
- `shamrock-from-crest.svg`: a pixel-contour vector derived from the centre of the existing `public/club-kits/panathinaikos/crest.png` in this repository. It is an approximation of the mark seen on this specific shirt, not an independent historic crest verification. Provenance of that existing crest is documented in `content/manual/kit-cof-panathinaikos.json` under `logo`.
- Shirt evidence: [Football Kit Archive, Panathinaikos 2024–25 home](https://www.footballkitarchive.com/panathinaikos-fc-2024-25-home-kit-271467/) and the existing local photo `public/kits/panathinaikos/fka-2024-25-home-271467.webp`. This is the home shirt with central white panel, green body, white collar and shoulder stripes, and Stoiximan. Alternative versions, competitions and later years must be checked separately.

Only `cof-k-56` selects these assets; they are not installed as global defaults. Identifying and checking marks, copyright and trademark permissions remain distinct from the owner's approval to use marks in the project. Do not infer that rights have been granted by a brand.
