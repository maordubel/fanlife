# Al Ahly — research brief
AHOFF and AHHON are club sources for live information and trophy categories; historical pages change. AHLY provides 235 Wikidata-derived player rows with imperfect and often incomplete dates and statistics, not an all-time complete roster. AS gives 2019/20 match rows; friendly and competition categories need checking. AH20 is the club's description of the 15-game African campaign; the earlier structured subset is included under prior_matches.csv. EG supplies Egyptian league winner years, while ACH gives additional trophy categories and seasons requiring validation. DERBY supplies dated historical Ahly–Zamalek games and lineups; extracted Ahly lineup strings preserve substitute annotations and must be resolved before a game can accept them.

CAF22 and AH20 tie the club to Wydad; CAF99 ties it to Raja. CULTA is an academic thesis on Ultras Ahlawy and Cairo fan practice. KAH supplies kit references. LIFE proposal: Cairo supporter routes across family, friends and matchday; validate locations and historical period locally. A sensitive history such as the 2012 Port Said tragedy requires careful source-led treatment and should not become a game challenge.
