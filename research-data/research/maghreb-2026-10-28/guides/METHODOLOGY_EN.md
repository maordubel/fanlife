# Method and use
The files are an evidence-linked research staging dataset, not a completed ClubData pack. Source dates 2026-10-28. All records have source IDs. A source may have incomplete or erroneous coverage. Source line numbers refer to text-rendered pages, are informative and may change. Historical periods, player name transliteration, positions and competition scope need independent cross-check. Years imported as strings can contain multiple spells or unknown ends. Do not treat one spelling as a canonical ID. Do not import incomplete periods as current contracts. Apps and goals from Wikidata-derived indices can refer to league only; may have gaps or wrong values. Separate senior competitive appearances from youth, reserves and friendlies.

The season matches cover selected seasons only, include friendlies and cup games, and need competition labels and deduplication before ingest. League champions sourced from RSSSF: the Morocco source includes pre-independence competitions, and the Egyptian page includes multiple sections. Retain competition era and verify titles individually. Source tables contain formatting artefacts; they are intentionally raw pending normalization. Historical derby lineups preserve bracketed substitutions; do not assume bracketed names started. Prior examples have their own prior_sources.csv identifiers.

Data rights: do not republish logos, player photos, music, video or kit images without determining rights. Raw factual data comes from named websites; ascertain licenses, attribution and permitted use before reuse. This package contains links and limited structured factual candidates, not a blanket license. A match report or names of scorers do not establish the movement needed to reconstruct a goal. Continue with canonical IDs, source-field evidence, dates and conflicts, multi-source checks, then review and publish per game readiness.
