# FanLife: Football Stories — art prompts for the film library

For the owner (2.11.2026). Every picture and clip below goes into the studio's library and is used by **any film of any
club** — the writer picks the right piece for each beat by its description, and the editor moves the camera over it,
lays the cards on it and darkens it where a card sits. One prompt per file. Copy the **style block** in front of every
prompt.

What already exists (do not order again): the Hapoel 1999/00 set (30 stills), the common set (10 stills — away coach,
boots on the touchline, flat-cap supporter, corner flag, radio on the table, archive trophy, manager at a blank board,
commentator booth, dugout, tunnel walk-out) and 5 clips (corner flag, stadium assembling, training ground, fan at the
TV, floodlights switching on).

**Batch 1 arrived the same day (2.11.2026)** and is in the library: every still in §4 (most in two variants, the second
keyed `…-b-kr` / `…-b-n`) and nine clips of §5. Held back, to be generated again:
- `signing-desk-kr` variant with the word "contract" printed on the paper (the other variant is in);
- `clip-scoreboard-flip-n` — a digit flashes on a panel mid-flip (the studio prints every score itself);
- `clip-pitch-builds-n` — the colour code "#15130F" is printed in the corners. **Never put a hex code in the prompt
  text for clips** — write the colours in words ("deep ink black, cream paper").
Still to make: `clip-stand-fills-kr`, `clip-clock-hands-n`, `clip-lights-dark-n`, every cut-out in §6, and the club sets
of §7.

## 1 · The style block (paste first, every time)

```
Handmade paper-cut collage diorama, layered cut paper with visible torn and cut edges, soft real shadows between
layers, halftone dot texture and newsprint scraps on some surfaces, matte cream paper (#F4ECD8) and deep ink black
(#15130F) as the main inks, mid-century sports-poster composition, cinematic 16:9 framing, gentle depth of field.
Figures are fictional, seen from behind, in silhouette or too small to recognise — no faces that could be a real person.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no club crests, no brand marks, no sponsor names, no flags of real countries.
```

## 2 · The colour rule — three kinds of piece

| Kind | Name suffix | Colours | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Any club (key red)** | `-kr` | ink + cream + greys + **one signal red `#C8102E`** for scarves, shirts, flags, seats | The studio repaints the red into each club's own colour (green for Celtic, blue for…). One picture serves every club. |
| **B · Neutral** | `-n` | ink + cream + greys only (no colour at all) | Safe on every page, including clubs whose rival owns red. |
| **C · One club** | `<club>-<season>-…` | that club's real colours | Only for a named club's own set. Never the rival's colour. |

Add to every **A** prompt: `The only colour in the image is signal red #C8102E, used for scarves, shirts, flags and seats;
everything else is cream, black and grey.`
Add to every **B** prompt: `Strictly monochrome: cream, black and greys only.`
Grass in A and B is **grey-olive paper, not green** (a green pitch would be greyed out on pages where green is the
rival's colour). No yellow, gold, orange or blue in A and B.

## 3 · Technical

- Stills: **16:9, at least 2752×1536** (1920×1080 minimum), JPG or PNG.
- Leave the **middle 60% calm** on pieces marked *card*: the score and the match card sit there.
- Clips: **1920×1080, 24 fps, 8–10 s**, no text, sound not needed. The camera is still or drifts slowly. **The last
  2 seconds hold still** (the film holds the last frame when a beat is longer than the clip).
- Cut-outs: **transparent PNG**, object only, no ground shadow baked in, 2000 px on the long side.
- File names: exactly the key in the tables below (e.g. `away-end-rain-kr.jpg`). Send as one ZIP per batch.

## 7 · One club's own set (kind C) — the template

For each film the studio needs ~25 pictures in the club's real colours, like the Hapoel 1999/00 set. Fill the
placeholders and run every line. Never use the rival's colour; no crests, no sponsors, no real players.

```
{CLUB_COLOURS}  e.g. green and white hoops        {CITY}   e.g. an industrial city of red-brick tenements
{GROUND}        e.g. a big oval ground with a covered terrace behind one goal
{ERA}           e.g. late 1960s — wool scarves, flat caps, wooden rattles, heavy leather ball
```

1. `<set>-ground-wide` — {GROUND} seen from the highest terrace step, a full crowd in {CLUB_COLOURS}, {ERA}.
2. `<set>-street-march` — supporters walking to {GROUND} through {CITY}, scarves in {CLUB_COLOURS}.
3. `<set>-terrace-roar` — the home end at the moment of a goal, arms up, {CLUB_COLOURS}.
4. `<set>-terrace-silent` — the same end silent after a goal against.
5. `<set>-goalmouth` — a forward in {CLUB_COLOURS} scoring at the home end.
6. `<set>-keeper` — the home goalkeeper diving, {ERA} kit.
7. `<set>-penalties` — a player in {CLUB_COLOURS} walking to the spot from behind.
8. `<set>-away-trip` — supporters in {CLUB_COLOURS} on the road / train / plane typical of {ERA}.
9. `<set>-away-end` — a small away end abroad with scarves in {CLUB_COLOURS}.
10. `<set>-derby-street` — the city split on derby day: two crowds on opposite pavements, one in {CLUB_COLOURS}, the other **grey only**.
11. `<set>-manager` — a manager in a coat on the touchline, from behind, {ERA}.
12. `<set>-dressing-room` — shirts in {CLUB_COLOURS} on pegs.
13. `<set>-trophy` — a plain silver cup on a table with a scarf in {CLUB_COLOURS}.

## 8 · Era modifiers (add one line when the story is in that decade)

- **1920s–40s**: wooden stands, cloth caps, rosettes, heavy laced ball, sepia newsprint.
- **1950s–60s**: wool scarves, rattles, floodlight pylons newly built, black-and-white television.
- **1970s–80s**: standing terraces, fences, flags on poles, transistor radios, long hair, tracksuits with stripes.
- **1990s**: all-seater conversions, baggy shirts, mobile phones the size of bricks, satellite dishes.
- **2000s–20s**: bowl stadiums, phone lights in the stands, tifo choreography, smartphones.

## 9 · Before you send a batch

- Nobody's face could be mistaken for a real person; no player numbers or names.
- No text, crest, sponsor, brand stripe or national flag anywhere (zoom in on shirts and boots).
- A-kind pieces: the only colour is the signal red. B-kind: no colour. Grass grey-olive.
- File named with its key; one ZIP per batch. The studio measures every file for rival colours on the way in.
