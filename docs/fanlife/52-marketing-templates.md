# DUBEL TEAM — design templates for the social plan

Every Telegram post ends with `Visual: T<n> …`. These are the eight templates it refers to, plus the
profile kit. Make each template once (Canva, Figma or an image model), then only swap the words, photo
and accent per post.

## The identity (shared by every template)

Paste this block at the top of every prompt below.

```
BRAND: DUBEL TEAM — one social page for five football apps (FAN LIFE, THE WORKER, DUBID, OFFSIDES, FUCK YOU).
FEEL: a 1970s football matchday programme printed on cheap paper, crossed with a modern sports app.
Two-colour screenprint look: slight misregistration of ~3px, halftone dots, paper grain. No gradients,
no glossy 3D, no stock-photo smiles, no default Canva look.
PALETTE: Ink #121412 · Paper #F2EDE3 · Vermilion #D8401C · Pitch green #1E5A38 · Night #0D1B2A.
APP ACCENT (one per post, used for one block only):
  FAN LIFE vermilion #D8401C · THE WORKER deep red #9E1B0E with navy #1C2B4A ·
  DUBID royal blue #2546C9 · OFFSIDES floodlight orange #EE7A1F · FUCK YOU pure black/white ·
  DUBEL TEAM (all apps) pitch green #1E5A38.
TYPE: headlines in a condensed poster grotesque (Anton or Archivo Black, all caps, tight leading);
body in Archivo Regular; small labels in Courier Prime, letter-spaced. English only.
FIXED ELEMENTS: small "DUBEL TEAM" wordmark top-left; the app name as a printed ticket-stub label
top-right; a thin perforated line above the footer; footer = app URL in Courier Prime.
SAFE ZONES: keep 120px clear at top and 220px at bottom on 9:16 formats (platform UI).
DON'T: no real club crests or player photos unless supplied; no real people's faces; no betting
imagery (no coins with €/$, no odds boards) for OFFSIDES — it is a free simulation.
```
