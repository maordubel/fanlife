## Where it lives

- Static pages: `/kit-archive` (hub) and `/kit-archive/<club>`; in-app: `/clubs/<club>/kit-archive`.
- Files: `public/club-kits/<club>/{cof,svg,icons,crest.png}`; data: `content/manual/kit-archive-<club>.json`, `kit-cof-<club>.json`; waves: `club-packs/<club>/wave-kits-*.json`.
- Features: `kitViews()` feeds gates 4 and 5, the market, Rumble, the closet and the auction.
