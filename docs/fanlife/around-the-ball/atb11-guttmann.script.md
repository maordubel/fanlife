# AROUND THE BALL · 11 · THE CURSE OF BÉLA GUTTMANN — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **WAR**. Status: **script ready**. Narrator: Kokoro George, Take B. Production: DOCU film + 3 Shorts + Reel + animated Short (see README).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "a 100-year curse" said as fact — whether Guttmann ever said it is itself disputed; told as the legend Benfica fans tell, Version A / Version B.
- Owner draft: "he asked for a raise and was thrown out" — the pay dispute is how the story is usually told; said as "the story goes".
- Count checked: eight European finals lost since 1962 — European Cup 1963, 1965, 1968, 1988, 1990; UEFA Cup 1983; Europa League 2013, 2014.

## Dossier

- Who: Béla Guttmann, Hungarian-Jewish coach. Benfica won the European Cup in 1961 and 1962 under him.
- The line attributed to him: not in a hundred years will Benfica be European champions.
