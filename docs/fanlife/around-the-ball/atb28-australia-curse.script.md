## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "32 years without the World Cup because of the curse" — Australia qualified for the 1974 World Cup after the curse of 1969. The 32 years run from 1974 to 2006. Corrected.
- Checked: 1969 qualifiers v Rhodesia in Mozambique; players say a witch doctor was hired and not paid in full; John Safran's 2004 TV visit; Australia qualified for 2006 (beating Uruguay on penalties, 16.11.2005).

## Script

| Beat | Narration |
|---|---|
| b01 | Mozambique, nineteen sixty-nine. Australia need to beat Rhodesia to reach the next round of World Cup qualifying. |
| b02 | The players, the story goes, hire a witch doctor. He buries bones by the goal. Australia win. |
| b03 | Then they do not pay him what they promised. And he curses them. |
| b04 | Australia did reach the World Cup — in nineteen seventy-four. Then nothing. For thirty-two years, every campaign ended in heartbreak. |
| b05 | In two thousand and four, a comedian, John Safran, flies to Mozambique for his TV show, finds a witch doctor, and has the curse lifted. A chicken is involved. |
| b06 | In November two thousand and five, Australia beat Uruguay on penalties. They are going to the World Cup. |
| b07 | Coincidence? Ask an Australian. |
