## Script

| Beat | Narration |
|---|---|
| b01 | Stuttgart, the twenty-second of June, two thousand and six. World Cup. Croatia against Australia. |
| b02 | In charge: Graham Poll, England's best referee, tipped by many to take the final. |
| b03 | Sixty-one minutes: yellow card for Croatia's Josip Šimunić. |
| b04 | Ninety minutes: Šimunić is booked again. Two yellows. That is a red. Except Poll does not show it. |
| b05 | Šimunić plays on. The final whistle goes. He argues with Poll. And Poll books him — a third time. Then shows red. |
| b06 | Poll's explanation: Šimunić was born in Australia and spoke with an Australian accent, and he had written the second card against an Australian number. |
| b07 | Poll was sent home from the World Cup. He retired from international refereeing. |
| b08 | Three yellow cards. One player. The World Cup's most famous piece of bookkeeping. |
