## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "within a week a historic ceasefire was declared" — not supported. The war did not end that night; a peace agreement came in 2007 (Ouagadougou). Said that way.
- Added (sourced): in 2007 Drogba helped take a national-team match to Bouaké, the rebel-held city.

## Dossier

- When: 8 October 2005 — Ivory Coast beat Sudan 3–1 in Khartoum and qualified for their first World Cup, after Cameroon drew with Egypt.
- What: in the dressing room, live on state television, Drogba and the players knelt and asked both sides to lay down their weapons and hold elections.
