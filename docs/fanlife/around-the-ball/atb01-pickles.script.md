# AROUND THE BALL · 01 · PICKLES — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **LAUGH**. About 420 narrated words (~3 min). Narrator: Kokoro George, Take B.

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: Pickles "ate from the players' plates" at the celebration — no source found; removed. Sources say he was invited to the banquet (and given a bone / a lick of a plate is folklore).
- Owner draft: the finder was alone — he was with his owner, David Corbett, who was briefly treated as a suspect.
- Added: the trophy was stolen again in 1983 in Rio de Janeiro and never recovered (the twist the close needs).

## Dossier

- **Where:** Methodist Central Hall, Westminster (the stamp exhibition, March 1966); Beulah Hill, Upper Norwood, south London (the find).
- **When:** stolen Sunday 20 March 1966; found Sunday 27 March 1966 — four months before England hosted the World Cup.
- **What:** the Jules Rimet Trophy, gold-plated, on show at "Stanley Gibbons Sporting Stamps".
