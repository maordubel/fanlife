## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "the players were drinking beer on beaches" — the holiday story is exaggerated in the telling; most of the squad were already back together for a friendly. Told as the myth, then the record.
- Checked: Yugoslavia excluded under UN sanctions about ten days before the tournament; Denmark beat the Netherlands on penalties in the semi-final and Germany 2–0 in the final (26 June 1992, Gothenburg).

- Where: Sweden, June 1992.
