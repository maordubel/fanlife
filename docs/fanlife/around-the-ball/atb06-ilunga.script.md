# AROUND THE BALL · 06 · THE FREE KICK — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **WAR** (the joke the world laughed at, and why it was not one). About 400 narrated words (~3 min).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: Mobutu threatened them "if they lost 4–0" — sources disagree on the threshold ("more than three", "4–0", "more than five"); said as "a heavy defeat".
- Owner draft: one explanation only — Ilunga himself gave two: fear of the regime, and (to L'Équipe) that he wanted a red card over a bonus dispute. Both told: Version A / Version B.

## Dossier

- **Where:** Parkstadion, Gelsenkirchen, West Germany. 1974 World Cup, Group 2.
- **When:** 22 June 1974. Brazil 3 Zaire 0. Zaire — the first sub-Saharan African side at a World Cup — had lost 2–0 to Scotland and 9–0 to Yugoslavia.
