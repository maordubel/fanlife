## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "Arsenal and Chelsea fought over him" — no source; removed.
- Owner draft: "he lifted defenders off the ground" — legend, unsourced; removed.
- Goals for Reading: 46 league goals in 121 league games (Wikipedia table). Said as "forty-six league goals".
- The policeman kiss: after his last-minute winner, 2–1 against Rochdale, April 1975 — no exact date in the source, so no day is said.
- "A builder" — the source says plasterer trainee, van driver, window cleaner, asphalter. Said as "an asphalter".
- "Twice player of the season" — not in the source; removed.
- The V-sign photograph: aimed at Luton goalkeeper Milija Aleksic (1977); the artwork of Super Furry Animals' 1996 single.

## Dossier

- **Who:** Robin Friday, 1952–1990. Forward. Reading 1974–1976, Cardiff City 1976–1977.
- **Retired:** 20 December 1977, aged 25. Died 22 December 1990, in Acton, aged 38.
- **2004:** voted the cult hero of both Reading and Cardiff City in a BBC poll.
- **Book:** *The Greatest Footballer You Never Saw* (Paolo Hewitt and Paul McGuigan, 1997).
