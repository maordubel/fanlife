# AROUND THE BALL · 05 · THE RAZOR IN THE GLOVE — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **WAR**. About 420 narrated words (~3 min).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: the flare "hit Rojas" — it did not; a photograph (El Gráfico) and video show it landed away from him.
- Owner draft: "banned for life, forever" — the life ban was lifted in 2001.
- Added: the woman who threw the flare, Rosenery Mello do Nascimento, was identified by Rio police — told without mockery.

## Dossier

- **Where:** Maracanã, Rio de Janeiro. 1990 World Cup qualifying, South America Group 3.
- **When:** 3 September 1989. Brazil led 1–0 (Careca, 49th minute per most reports — minute not said on screen). Chile needed a win.
- **Outcome:** match abandoned; FIFA awarded Brazil 2–0; Chile banned from the 1994 qualifiers.
