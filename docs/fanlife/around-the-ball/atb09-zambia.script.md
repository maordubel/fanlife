# AROUND THE BALL · 09 · THE BEACH AT LIBREVILLE — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **HEART**. Quiet; no shout words, no stings on the crash. About 380 narrated words (~3 min).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: final on 13 February — the final was **12 February 2012** (Zambia celebrated into the 13th).
- Owner draft: the whole team died — 18 players died, with the coach, staff and crew (30 people on board). Kalusha Bwalya and the players based in Europe were travelling separately.
- Owner draft: "the stadium is on the crash site" — the Stade d'Angondjé is a few kilometres from the place where the plane came down off the coast. Said that way.

## Dossier

- **The crash:** 27 April 1993. A Zambian Air Force Buffalo, on the way to a World Cup qualifier in Senegal, crashed into the sea shortly after taking off from Libreville, Gabon.
- **The final:** 12 February 2012, Stade d'Angondjé (Stade de l'Amitié), Libreville. Zambia 0 Ivory Coast 0 after extra time; Zambia won 8–7 on penalties. Coach: Hervé Renard. Didier Drogba missed a penalty in normal time; Stoppila Sunzu scored the winning kick.
- **The week:** the squad laid flowers on the beach near the crash site before the final, with Kalusha Bwalya, by then FAZ president.
