# AROUND THE BALL · 03 · THE SEVEN CATS — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **WAR** (a curse told as a case). About 430 narrated words (~3 min).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "Racing won nothing for 35 years" — Racing won the 1988 Supercopa Sudamericana; what they did not win for 35 years was the **Argentine league** (last 1966, next the 2001 Apertura). Said exactly that way.
- Owner draft: one fixed version of the burial — the sources say the story "changes every time you read it" (date 1967 to the 1970s; cats or frogs). Told as a legend, with Version A / Version B.
- Exorcism year: 1998 (Bitbol) or 1999 (WSC) — said as "at the end of the nineties".

## Dossier

- **Where:** Avellaneda, Buenos Aires. Racing's El Cilindro (Estadio Presidente Perón) and Independiente's ground are about 300 metres apart.
- **When:** the legend begins with Racing's 1967 Intercontinental Cup win over Celtic; ends with the 2001 Apertura (December 2001).
