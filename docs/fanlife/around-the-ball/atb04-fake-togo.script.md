## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "a team of taxi drivers" — no source names who the players were; they are "unidentified". Removed.
- Owner draft: Bana "jailed" — sources say he was banned for three years by Togo's federation and later described by the gendarmerie as "on the run". No conviction found.
- The link to the fixer Wilson Raj Perumal is said as "has since been linked", not as a finding.

## Dossier

- **Where:** Bahrain National Stadium, Riffa.
- **When:** Tuesday 7 September 2010. Togo's real team had played in Botswana three days earlier (lost 2–1, Nations Cup qualifier).
