# AROUND THE BALL · 25 · THE FAN WHO CAME ON — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **LAUGH**. Status: **script ready**. Narrator: Kokoro George, Take B. Production: DOCU film + 3 Shorts + Reel + animated Short (see README).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "Redknapp the manager" — in July 1994 Harry Redknapp was West Ham's assistant manager (he became manager that August). Corrected.
- Owner draft: "his goal was ruled offside" — Redknapp's account and later reports say he scored. Told as Version A (it counted) / Version B (it did not); no footage exists.
- Checked: pre-season friendly at Oxford City, July 1994; Davies replaced Lee Chapman; Redknapp told the announcer he was a Bulgarian, "Tittyshev".

## Dossier

- Source of record: Jeff Maysh, Howler / The Guardian (5.9.2013).

## Script

| Beat | Narration |
|---|---|
| b01 | Oxford, July nineteen ninety-four. A pre-season friendly. West Ham are away at Oxford City. |
| b02 | By the dugout, a West Ham fan, Steve Davies, has had a few beers. He spends the half telling the bench that the striker, Lee Chapman, is useless. |
| b03 | Harry Redknapp turns round. Can you play as well as you talk? |
| b04 | He can find out. Davies is taken to the dressing room and given a kit. In the second half, Chapman comes off. Steve Davies comes on. |
| b05 | The local announcer asks who the new man is. Redknapp tells him: the Bulgarian. Tittyshev. |
| b06 | Did he score? Version A: yes, and he ran round like he had won the World Cup. Version B: it did not count. There is no film. |
| b07 | Either way, a man walked out of the crowd and played for the club he loved. |
