# AROUND THE BALL · 02 · 149–0 — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **LAUGH** (told straight). About 380 narrated words (~2.5 min).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "the players were banned for life" — sources name bans for the SOE coach and players for between one and three years; "for life" is not supported. Told as "suspended", with the coach named.
- Added: the cause — a disputed late penalty in SOE's previous match, a 2–2 draw with DSA Antananarivo, which handed the title to AS Adema.

## Dossier

- **Where:** Toamasina, Madagascar. Championnat National (THB Champions League), play-off round.
- **When:** 31 October 2002.
- **Record:** the highest scoreline in a recorded football match (Guinness World Records). All 149 goals are own goals.
