# AROUND THE BALL · 07 · THE FOG — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **LAUGH** (history underneath). About 420 narrated words (~3 min).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "a fan came down and replaced the keeper" — no source; removed.
- Owner draft: "Dynamo played with twelve men" — reported by some accounts (FourFourTwo via Flashbak), unverified; said as "some accounts say".
- Half-time: Arsenal led 3–2 (Wikipedia; Arsenal history pages say 3–1 — the 3–2 from the Wikipedia season page is used, with the count on screen held at "ARSENAL AHEAD").

## Dossier

- **Where:** White Hart Lane, Tottenham — Arsenal's wartime home; Highbury was still an Air Raid Precautions centre.
- **When:** Wednesday 21 November 1945. Thick fog, visibility about 40 metres (Dynamo's own account). Crowd around 54,000.
- **Referee:** Nikolai Latyshev of Moscow, with both linesmen on the same side.
- **Arsenal:** strengthened with guest players, among them Stanley Matthews and Stan Mortensen.
