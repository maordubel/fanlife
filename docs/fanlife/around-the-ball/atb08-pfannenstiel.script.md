# AROUND THE BALL · 08 · THE MAN WHO PLAYED EVERYWHERE — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **LAUGH**. About 380 narrated words (~2.5 min).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "he was convicted of match-fixing in Singapore" — sources conflict (convicted vs. cleared on appeal); he has always protested his innocence. Said as: held for 101 days on match-fixing charges; he has always said he was innocent.
- Owner draft: "he died three times" — he stopped breathing three times and was revived (26 Dec 2002). Said that way.
- The penguin: his own story; told as "he says".

## Dossier

- **Who:** Lutz Pfannenstiel, German goalkeeper, born 1973. The only player known to have played professionally in all six FIFA confederations.
- **Bradford:** Bradford Park Avenue v Harrogate Town, Boxing Day 2002 — collision, lung collapse, revived three times.
- **Later:** a scout and football director (Hoffenheim, then St. Louis City SC).
