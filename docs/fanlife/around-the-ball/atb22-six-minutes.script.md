# AROUND THE BALL · 22 · THE SIX-MINUTE MATCH — script (draft 1, 10.11.2026)

Tone: **WAR**. Status: **script ready**. Narrator: Kokoro George, Take B. Production: DOCU film + 3 Shorts + Reel + animated Short (see README).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: 80,000 — reports say about 70,000 were evacuated. Owner draft: "Zidane won a penalty and scored" — Ronaldo won it (fouled by Labaka); Zidane scored it.
- Checked: stopped at 87–88' with 1–1 on 12 December 2004 after a warning via Gara attributed to ETA; no bomb found; the last minutes plus four added were played on 5 January 2005.

## Dossier

- Where: Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid.

## Script

| Beat | Narration |
|---|---|
| b01 | Madrid, the twelfth of December, two thousand and four. Real Madrid against Real Sociedad. One, one. Three minutes left. |
| b02 | A warning reaches a Basque newspaper: a bomb will go off at the stadium. |
| b03 | The Bernabéu is emptied in minutes. About seventy thousand people, onto the streets. The players leave in their kit. |
| b04 | No bomb is found. But the game cannot just end. So the federation decides: the last minutes will be played later. |
| b05 | The fifth of January, two thousand and five. The stadium fills again. For about six minutes of football. |
| b06 | Ronaldo runs into the box and is brought down. Penalty. Zidane scores. Two, one. |
| b07 | The shortest match in La Liga history — and it was decided in it. |
