# FAN LIFE social — art round 2 (prompts) · updated 27.10.2026

**Status (27.10.2026, evening): DELIVERED — all 61 files are in the kit.** Gate plates draw on every L-GATE post, covers on the first carousel slide, moments in the T1 window and on the FAN LIFE safety-net card. Stickers and textures are in the kit and not drawn yet.

Every file: **PNG, transparent background**, the exact name shown, into `scripts/marketing/kit/assets/` (or attached
in chat — I place it). Nothing goes live until it is in the kit and you have seen a test post.

**House style for everything below:** a 1970s British comic football weekly (Shoot!, Scorcher, Roy of the Rovers),
ink drawing with a halftone tone layer. Two layers per drawing: `-line` (pure black ink on transparent) and `-tone`
(one flat mid-grey halftone fill on transparent, registered exactly under the line). The kit colours the tone in the
club's own colour, so **no colour in the drawing**. No real player, no real face, no club crest, no sponsor or maker
logo, no text inside the drawing (stickers in §4 are the only exception).
