# FAN LIFE social — art round 2 (prompts)

What exists today, and what is already used in every post, is in §0. §1–§6 are the prompts for the next round: each
one is self-contained, copy it as is into the image tool. Every file comes back as **PNG with a transparent
background, 2048 px on the long side**, and goes into `scripts/marketing/kit/assets/` under the name shown.

## 0 · Already in the kit (no need to redraw)

- FAN LIFE badge (`assets/brand/fanlife-logo.png`) — top-left of every FAN LIFE post since 27.10.2026.
- Legends Manager badge (`assets/brand/legends-logo.png`) — on every Legends Manager post.
- DUBID and OFFSIDES logos (`assets/brand/dubid-logo.png`, `offsides-logo.png`).
- Eight comic plates (`kicker`, `keeper`, `dribbler`, `fans`, `memo`, `scarf`, `swap`, `bootball`), line + tone.
- Club crests: the shared crest library (ecosystem/crests), printed in their own colours.
- Club colours: worn the way each shirt wears them (stripes, hoops, sash, halves, checks, or a sunburst).

**House style for everything below:** a 1970s British comic football weekly (Shoot!, Scorcher, Roy of the Rovers),
drawn in ink with a halftone tone layer. Two layers per drawing: a `-line` file (black ink only, pure black on
transparent) and a `-tone` file (one flat mid-grey halftone fill on transparent, registered exactly under the line).
The kit colours the tone in the club's own colour, so **no colour in the drawing itself**. No real player, no real
face, no club crest, no sponsor or maker logo, no text inside the drawing.
