## The flow

1. **Brief** (desk) — club, season, length (0.5–25 min), visibility, optional scheduled time (Athens). Saved as a job in
   the durable store (`stories/jobs/<id>.json`, compare-and-swap).
2. **Worker** (`.github/workflows/stories-worker.yml`, every 10 min) asks `/api/master/stories/worker` (`CRON_SECRET`):
   `peek` → `claim` (6.5 h lease) → `progress` (pause/cancel answered on every report) → `upload_urls` → `finish`.
3. **Pipeline** (`scripts/football-stories/studio/`, Python):
   research ledger (archive + dossier claims, every row with a source) → script (Claude / OpenAI / Gemini, validated:
   every number in narration and on screen must come from a cited claim) → voice (ElevenLabs if the key exists, else
   free Kokoro `bm_george`) → graphics (Playwright cards in The Weekly's type) → edit (paper collage, camera moves,
   owner cut-outs, owner beat illustrations) → audio (synthesised score, owner crowd recordings, −14 LUFS) → master,
   preview, captions, chapters, description with sources, 3 thumbnails → QA (streams, sync, loudness, black, captions,
   rival colours, contact sheet).
4. **Review** (desk) — the script, the preview, thumbnails, checks, the YouTube package. Approve, retry, cancel.
5. **Upload** — through **YouTube Studio**, never the API (API uploads from an unaudited project are locked private). The
   desk records only what Studio shows: started → done (video id + visibility) or uncertain.

## Other clubs (2.11.2026)

Every club but Hapoel Tel Aviv reads its compiled FAN LIFE pack (`content/compiled/<club>.json`, built by
`npm run clubs:precompile`): approved rows with an https source and confidence 2, the season taken from the date
(July–June). Competitions carry the name they had THEN — UEFA files every UEFA Cup tie as the Europa League, the film
says UEFA Cup before 2009/10. Scorers print only when the record names every goal. A second leg knows whether the club
went through (aggregate, then away goals) and stamps THROUGH! or OUT instead of WIN/LOST. Map pins come from
`dossiers/places.json`; a domestic map highlights the club's own country. Film 2: Celtic 2002/03, `dossiers/celtic-2002-03*`.

## Beat art (owner illustrations)

`studio/kit/beats/<club id>-<season>/` — colour paper-cut pictures made for one story, with `index.json` (key,
description, source file, sha). A script names them as `"art"` or `"backdrop": "beat:<set>/<slug>"`; a key from another
club's set is refused. Colours are kept; any pixel in the club's rival family is scrubbed at render time (`paper.scrub`),
and behind a card the picture is darkened. An owner picture switches off the automatic cut-out props on that shot.
The writer is shown the set's descriptions, so a new film of the same season picks from them by itself.

Hapoel Tel Aviv 1999/00: 30 pieces, supplied 2.11.2026 (AI-generated by the owner, fictional figures from behind).

**Common set** (`beat:common/…`, `clip:common/…`) serves every club. Each piece lists the colour families it prints;
the writer is not offered a piece whose family the club's rival owns. A piece marked `"accent": "key-red"` is drawn with
one signal red (#C8102E) and the studio repaints that red into the club's own colour (`paper.recolor`).

**Cut-outs** (`studio/kit/cutouts/common/*.webp`, `cut:common/…`): figures, crowd rows and close-ups with alpha,
cut from the owner's white or paper backgrounds by `cutout.py`. Each carries a `motion` (run · dive · jump · rise · pop ·
row). A script may name them as `"figure"` on any visual; otherwise the editor chooses by the beat — a win brings a
celebrating player, a defeat a kneeling one, a shoot-out a diving keeper, a matchday a crowd row along the bottom.
People stand BEHIND the card, crowd rows rise in FRONT. Variants (`-b-`, `-c-`) are picked by club and shot, so films of
different clubs do not repeat a drawing; key red is repainted into the club's colour.

**Captions** are burned in word by word (`captions.py`); the YouTube caption track is delivered as well. **Every film ends
on the site** (`script.with_cta`): the narrator's closing line and the end card with the badge, six feature tiles and the
address.

**Clips** (`studio/kit/clips/<set>/*.mp4`, silent h.264, ~10 s): a moving world. Decoded in order at 25 fps, scrubbed and
recoloured per frame, played once and held on the last frame.

New batches: `python3 scripts/football-stories/ingest-art.py <zip> [--set <club>-<season>]` (names are the keys in
`docs/fanlife/55-football-stories-art-prompts.md`).

## Rules

- No fake progress, uploads or success: every state is reported by the worker or by YouTube Studio.
- Every number is sourced (`fact_guard`); a scorer is printed only when the archive names every goal.
- No real person's face; illustrations are symbolic and the description says so.
- Every film asks once to follow the channel (CLAUDE.md rule 136, art in doc 58).
- Rival colours (rule 95) are checked on sampled frames of every film.
