## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft presented Maradona's visit as fact — corrected: it is HIS account, and he also denied ever meeting Escobar (Olé). Both versions kept.
- Owner draft quotes "I thought he was a tycoon who sold a lot of ice" and Escobar's "we both came from poverty" — the second is reported (AS via Pulzo) and may be used only as "he said Escobar told him"; the first has no source and was removed.
- Owner draft: "Escobar's team won, of course" — no source; removed. "Golden taps, jacuzzi" — jacuzzi is reported; golden taps not; removed.
- Escobar escaped in July 1992 and was killed in December 1993 — both standard record; added to close the file.
