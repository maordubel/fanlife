## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft: "three armed men" — the sources say two men at gunpoint took him; three men were in the plot.
- Owner draft: the Schuster line "I can't play with a gun at my friend's head" — no source; replaced with his recorded words, "As well as legs, I have a heart."
- Owner draft: "Barcelona lost game after game" — corrected to the record: one draw in four league games.
- Owner draft: "a Swiss account, arrested in Geneva" — the sources say the money trail and a bank withdrawal led police to him; the city of the arrest is not consistent across sources and is left out.
- Kept: 1 March 1981, 6–0 v Hércules, two goals, 25 days, Zaragoza, 350 → 100 million pesetas, freed 25 March, forgave them, Copa del Rey that June.
