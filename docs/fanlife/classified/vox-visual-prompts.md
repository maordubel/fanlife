# CLASSIFIED · VOX visual production prompts

Use the episode scene IDs as filenames. Each prompt below is for a new illustration or graphic layer. Original photographs stay intact as archive stills. The matching file in the project’s Drive is the first reference for framing, colours and objects. Add the labels, names, dates, arrows and numbers in the editor on separate layers.

## Shared setup

- Export a 1920 × 1080 background and separate transparent PNG foregrounds. For a 9:16 crop, keep the main subject and the reveal within the central 48% of the frame. Leave the lower quarter clear for captions and a quiet corner for the source tag.
- Build three depth layers. Use a wide hold first, a single camera move to the detail that matters, then a brief hold for the viewer to read it. Move one object or marker at a time. Place cuts and sound accents on a word in the recorded narration.
- Use paper grain, black ink, small source tags and restrained colour plates. Keep the supplied club identity colours on the right teams. Make scoreboard and table graphics editable. Export all imagery without numbers, lettering, fake newspaper headlines, invented logos or broadcast watermarks.
- Label historical illustrations **RECONSTRUCTION** on screen. For a supplied photo whose scene or date is uncertain, use **CONTEXT PHOTO** and a supported caption. An original still can pan or zoom within its frame. Keep people and objects in the still fixed.

|---|---|---|
| Opening fixture | **Wide horizontal editorial collage**, 2002 tournament era. Empty tunnel opens onto an overcast stadium, with a match ticket and two blank nameplates in the foreground. Three isolated passes for bottle, bench and shadow. No hands or labels. | Cut from the stadium wide to the object close-up. | Two accounts | **Split evidence board**, two equal blank paper strips, separate source pins and a central divider, each side with a neutral human silhouette in profile. | Resolution | **A closing dossier**, folder on a bench, ticket partly visible beneath the lid, stadium light falling across the paper. Isolated lid, ticket, light and shadows. | Close the folder on the final narrative turn. Hold the real source card before the CTA. |
