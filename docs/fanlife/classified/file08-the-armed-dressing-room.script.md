# CLASSIFIED · FILE 08 · THE ARMED DRESSING ROOM — script for approval (draft 1, 8.11.2026)

From the owner's Hebrew treatment of 8.11.2026, written in English for the channel and checked against the sources below. Tab: **WAR**. Narrator: Kokoro George, Take B. About 254 narrated words (~1.8 min).

## What the fact-check changed

- Owner draft named the second clan's leader as "Luigi Martini"; the sources found speak of Chinaglia's group and the group opposed to it — the narration names Chinaglia and Re Cecconi only.
- Owner draft: "the management turned off the corridor lights so they would not shoot the bulbs" — told only as players' stories, not as record.
- Owner draft: "Re Cecconi raised his collar, hand in pocket, shouted 'this is a robbery'" — this is the version of the time; Ghedin, the family and a 2012 book say he never said it. Both kept.
- Owner draft: "a jeweller friend" — corrected: the friend was Fraticcioli, who knew the jeweller.
