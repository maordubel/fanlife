# FanLife: Football Stories — art round 4: the complete library (4.11.2026)

For the owner. Rounds 1–3 (docs 55, 56, 64) cover the scenes, figures, crowd rows, transitions, puppets, depth layers,
overlays and the CLASSIFIED dossier. This round fills every remaining gap, so any story — any club, any decade, any
country, a documentary, a CLASSIFIED file or an I WAS THERE vlog — finds a fitting picture without a new order.

Use the **style blocks, colour kinds (`-kr` one signal red / `-n` no colour) and the transparency rule of doc 64 §0**.
Never a hex code, never letters or digits in a picture. Sizes: scenes 16:9 at the largest size, cut-outs 2048 px.
Delivery: `02_סטודיו_דוקומנטרי_ונכסים/Art_Round_4/<section>/` with the file names below, then write "עלה".

Order of value: **§1 recurring supporter · §2 grounds by era · §3 cities · §4 people · §5 inserts · §6 matchday
objects · §7 documents · §8 maps · §9 weather · §10 scrapbook kit (I WAS THERE) · §11 transitions · §12 series plates.**

## 1 · The Supporter — one recurring character for the whole channel

One fictional supporter who appears in every film, always from behind or in silhouette: the viewer's eyes on the
story. Same person in every picture (attach the first good one as the reference for the rest).

```
[Documentary style block] THE SUPPORTER: one fictional man in his forties, medium build, short dark hair seen from
behind, a dark wool coat and a long scarf in signal red, always seen from behind or in silhouette — never his face.
[POSE]. Isolated on a transparent background, full body. Same character as the attached reference.
```
