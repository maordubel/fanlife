# Football Stories — the playbook (3.11.2026)

What the first four films taught, written as rules the studio now follows. Owner's brief (3.11.2026): faster, more
gripping, more emotional, football as a religion, famous quotes, press of the day, source quotes, conclusions.

## 1. Choose a story, not a season

A season film is a list; a tie is a story. The strongest material has **a turn**: a deficit, a ban, an empty ground, a
date that comes back. Brief it with a **focus** window (both legs, the run-in, the final week) and let the archive count
only what happened inside it. Olympiacos 2023/24 → v Maccabi Tel Aviv: 1–4 at home in front of 31,054; 4–1 away in front
of 370; four months later, the first European trophy of a Greek club.

## 2. Open cold, close on the people

- The first ten seconds are the worst moment or the strangest number (370 people). Context comes after the hook.
- End on supporters, not on a scoreline: who travelled, who waited, what it meant. The follow line (rule 136) closes it.

## 3. Pace is a number

- 148 words a minute, Kokoro at speed 1.0, 0.22 s lead and 0.32 s tail per beat, 0.95 s at a chapter turn.
- At the big moments one sentence per beat; goal by goal with the minute and the aggregate after it.
- Plant early, pay off late (a coach's line after the first leg is answered by the second leg's minute).

## 4. Football is a faith — say it with sources

- One famous line where the story turns (Shankly, voted the most memorable football quote — Reuters 2006), printed word
  for word, speaker and source on the card.
- The people inside the story speak in their own words: coach after the first leg, captain after the second.
- The press of the day as a headline card in our type, outlet and date, never a copied masthead.
- No invented quote, ever. A line the owner found goes into the brief with its link; the script can print only that.

## 5. Pictures

- Owner illustrations drawn for the story beat the generic kit: a header, a knee-slide, the away end.
- Photoreal pictures carry ILLUSTRATION on screen and YES in YouTube's altered-content answer. Tickets and newspapers are
  blurred where a person or a masthead would be legible.
- Name every file after what it shows — the writer chooses by the name.

## 6. Exposure

- Title: the gap in numbers ("Three Goals Down at Home. 370 People Watching. Then This."). Ten candidates are generated;
  pick the one a supporter of the club would send to a friend.
- Thumbnail: one face of emotion or one number, large, the club colour.
- Description: a two-line hook, chapters, sources, links. Tags in the publish kit.
- **Shorts are the trailer**: 2–3 per film, ≤58 s, each one moment with a hook in the first second, the film linked in the
  first comment. They are cut automatically and appear in the desk.
- Playlist "Seasons We Never Forget"; end screen to the next film; a card to the club's earlier film at the midpoint.
- Publish the film first, the Shorts over the following days.

## 7. The desk does the rest

Brief → (pictures) → film + Shorts → publish kit. Every number still comes from a sourced claim; every state is the
worker's or YouTube Studio's. Doc 54 is the machinery.
