# Every Football Stories film is assembled in the model's order, and ships as the model's package (owner, 13.11.2026)

Owner, on the AEK 0–1 Panathinaikos example (7.11.1994): *"Take it as the assembly model for all our films
automatically. I want a full package: the opening, a smart ending, the HOOK, REELS, SHORTS, an automatic marketing
package for social."*

- **The order.** Ident (≤ 3 s) → hook (one close picture a sentence) → the film's question as a full-screen card before
  0:15, the result withheld → title sting → chapters that each end on a turn → the decisive moment as a mini-drama
  (clock, held breath, release) → payoff in explainers → the opening card again, answered → one or two sourced last
  pages → the next film named and one subscribe ask → end card.
- **The package.** film (16:9, 1080p) with captions, the gate's report and a contact sheet somebody looked at, two
  thumbnails measured at 168×94, three Shorts that open a loop and send to the film, two Reels that stand alone, the
  marketing pack (titles, description with chapters and sources, pinned comment, captions per platform, a seven-day
  schedule in Europe/Athens time) and the claim ledger.
- **Facts first.** Two independent publishers for every line said or shown, or it is cut or softened; what was cut is
  listed with its reason (rules 2, 11, 16, 18).
- **The owner's pictures stay out of git**; the storyboard names them and `--pack` assembles them with the library
  pieces chosen through the media index.
- The recipe, the specs and the gap list against the automatic pipeline: `docs/fanlife/74-model-film.md` and the
  model film `scripts/football-stories/studio/docu/films/pao_aek_1994.py`. Rule 142 stands through all of it.
