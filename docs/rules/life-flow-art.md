# LIFE has its own picture order in Flow (owner, 23.11.2026)

- **The owner's instruction, in meaning (23.11.2026):** use Flow wherever it is needed to make LIFE a more real experience: the streets of Athens, of Tel Aviv, grounds that recall the originals, and the story tied closely to what is seen.
- **What the order is:** `scripts/life/flow/life-library.ts` writes `scripts/life/flow/life-library.json` (77 pieces in the first order, kept lean because Flow credits are limited): building facades per town, front-on and edge to edge, to map on a wall; one ground plate per core club (two for Hapoel Tel Aviv, Olympiacos, Panathinaikos, AEK); a skyline and a straight-down district per town; plates for the childhood chapters. Each has a `priority` and the operator draws the lowest number first.
- **The look, in every prompt:** photoreal, blue-hour dusk after rain, wet ground, warm street lamps (the visual floor, `docs/life/visual-target-2026-11-23/`). Not the films' red-and-cream print.
- **Hard negatives, in every prompt:** no text, letters, numbers or written signs; no logos, crests, sponsor marks, advertising or flags; no real person's likeness (people from behind or far only); no yellow paint or clothing. No prompt names a club, a ground, a person or a brand. A ground plate keeps seats and trim neutral grey and concrete, so it can never wear a rival's colour.
- **A ground "recalls the original" only where we know which ground it is:** its general, well-known character is described when the club's `world.json` names the ground the lines were written for; otherwise the plate is a neutral city ground marked `confidence: generic`. The descriptions are general knowledge, not sourced facts, and they are never printed to a supporter.
- **Storage:** the private bucket, `library/life/<stem>.png`, stems `^life-[a-z0-9-]{2,70}-fl\d$` (no `-kr`: never recoloured). Read by `lib/life/flowArt.ts`, which answers empty when storage refuses.
- **The films' art library is untouched:** `library/flow`, `LIB_STEM`, `studio/cinema/flow_library.json` and their tests do not change, and a LIFE piece is never picked by the cinema.
- **Order in the hourly run** (doc 70 §LIFE's picture order): CLASSIFIED panels, then a film's `person-` pieces, then up to 6 LIFE pieces, then up to 6 art-library pieces. Stills only, so `flow-no-films` holds.
- Changing a prompt means a new stem (`-fl2`); the test `tests/life-flow-art.test.ts` holds the spec to the generator.
- **Baked for the game (24.11.2026):** workflow `life-flow-bake` (`scripts/life/flow/bake.py`) turns `library/life` into `public/life/flow/<stem>-<size>.webp` plus, for facades, `-n-512` / `-r-512` / `-e-512` (normal, roughness, lit windows) and `manifest.json` (sizes, bytes, storeys, colour-family shares, `use` + `note` from review). A piece reviewed `use:false` is never shown. Readers go through `lib/life/rush-render/look/flowArt.ts` (`flowPieces`, `flowUrl`); the street is dressed by `look/flowStreet.ts` (facades on the main street and on every side street, a facade key that is a word placed by its role — `shop`/`cafe` over a ground floor, `corner`/`pilotis` as a building —, the ground's plate, the skyline band; THE WORKER's Tel Aviv kiosk front, old gates, junction reflections, mist and haze), lazily after the first frame and failing soft. The cron only fires once the workflow is on `main`.
- The current order has 97 pieces. The Athens and Tel Aviv street modules cover shops, kiosks, cafes, corner and pilotis buildings, and balcony strips. Zrinjski, Celtic, St. Pauli and Atalanta ground plates have priority 2.
- **The key people (28.11.2026):** use `cast`, stems `life-cast-<people>-<who>-fl1`, 36 portraits (twelve people, the family and the street's own types: the hard lad of the terrace, the boy with the album, the one who makes everybody laugh; × three parts of the world: `med`, `north`, `maghreb`; `TOWN_PEOPLE` says which a town is). Their own light (`CAST_STYLE`: a street lamp's warm key, a cool fill, a plain grey backdrop) instead of the wet street; every other negative holds, and each is an invented face that resembles no real individual. Baked at 768 and 384; read by `castFace` (`lib/life/hood/flowRef.ts`).
