# LIFE has its own picture order in Flow (owner, 23.11.2026)

- **The owner's instruction, in meaning (23.11.2026):** use Flow wherever it is needed to make LIFE a more real experience: the streets of Athens, of Tel Aviv, grounds that recall the originals, and the story tied closely to what is seen.
- **What the order is:** `scripts/life/flow/life-library.ts` writes `scripts/life/flow/life-library.json` (87 pieces in the first order since Istanbul and Belgrade joined on 24.11.2026, at most 90, kept lean because Flow credits are limited; the new towns sit at the lowest priority): building facades per town, front-on and edge to edge, to map on a wall; one ground plate per core club (two for Hapoel Tel Aviv, Olympiacos, Panathinaikos, AEK); a skyline and a straight-down district per town; plates for the childhood chapters. Each has a `priority` and the operator draws the lowest number first.
- **The look, in every prompt:** photoreal, blue-hour dusk after rain, wet ground, warm street lamps (the visual floor, `docs/life/visual-target-2026-11-23/`). Not the films' red-and-cream print.
- **Hard negatives, in every prompt:** no text, letters, numbers or written signs; no logos, crests, sponsor marks, advertising or flags; no real person's likeness (people from behind or far only); no yellow paint or clothing. No prompt names a club, a ground, a person or a brand. A ground plate keeps seats and trim neutral grey and concrete, so it can never wear a rival's colour.
- **A ground "recalls the original" only where we know which ground it is:** its general, well-known character is described when the club's `world.json` names the ground the lines were written for; otherwise the plate is a neutral city ground marked `confidence: generic`. The descriptions are general knowledge, not sourced facts, and they are never printed to a supporter.
- **Storage:** the private bucket, `library/life/<stem>.png`, stems `^life-[a-z0-9-]{2,70}-fl\d$` (no `-kr`: never recoloured). Read by `lib/life/flowArt.ts`, which answers empty when storage refuses.
- **The films' art library is untouched:** `library/flow`, `LIB_STEM`, `studio/cinema/flow_library.json` and their tests do not change, and a LIFE piece is never picked by the cinema.
- **Order in the hourly run** (doc 70 §LIFE's picture order): CLASSIFIED panels, then a film's `person-` pieces, then up to 6 LIFE pieces, then up to 6 art-library pieces. Stills only, so `flow-no-films` holds.
- Changing a prompt means a new stem (`-fl2`); the test `tests/life-flow-art.test.ts` holds the spec to the generator.
