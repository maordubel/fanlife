# Quality first, FAN LIFE on every piece, the daily doctor (owner, 14.11.2026)

"Front / back / different prints from the opening of a product. The supplier's better products by default — quality above all. Automatic fitting for the best print in size, layout, puzzle, notebook. Help me make the best product. Categories in editing. Fix my mistakes once a day. Every product as branded FAN LIFE as possible — a fashion brand."

- **Builder in four steps** (Create): the picture → what to make → **how it prints** (front only · back only · front + back same · front + back two pictures, with the back's own picture from the library or an upload; `ProductDesign.backArt`) → finish (optional: size, place, colours, background, margin). The same "How it prints" control is in Edit print.
- **Quality-first blanks and printers** (`KIND_SPECS.find`, `PROVIDER_ORDER`): Printify Choice first (top-rated printers under Printify's quality promise), then the European printers; premium blanks first where Printify has them (garment-dyed tee, premium pullover hoodie, fine-art / giclée posters, 1.25″ canvas). Matches are kept; Settings → "Pick the best blanks again" clears them for the next press.
- **Size by size** (`bestVariants`): each variant's sharpness is worked out for the picture (fitted whole); a full-bleed product keeps the variants in the picture's shape; kept are the sizes at ≥ 50% of the pixels they need, else those above the 20% floor, else the sharpest one. A puzzle drops the piece counts the picture cannot carry; a poster the sizes it cannot fill.
- **Wrap-around covers** (journals, notebooks: one landscape sheet = back + spine + front): the art goes whole on the front half on its own paper colour (`wrapCover`), never across the spine.
- **FAN LIFE on every garment** (`BRAND_SPOTS`, Settings → "FAN LIFE on every piece"): the seal on the inside neck label, the left sleeve and under the collar on the back, wherever the printer has the place and the design does not use it. FAN LIFE's own products carry the colour seal; a club's carry it in one ink (`seal-mono-512.png`) so a rival's colour never rides on a club's product. The extra areas' cost is in the price automatically.
- **Categories**: a product sits in its own category and any others ticked in Edit (`ShopProduct.shelves`); the storefront and category-scoped sales follow.
- **The daily doctor** (`lib/shop/doctor.ts`, once per Athens day from the hourly check-up, or "Run the doctor now"): fixes titles, hides duplicates (newer stays), re-presses one draft-or-soft product a day; lists what needs the owner (file-like design names, paper ground on dark garments, designs on fewer than three products). Never deletes, never shows what was hidden.
- **Sales coach** (Overview): hidden-but-sellable products, no sale, no favourites, empty ribbon, empty categories, no back prints — each with its button.
