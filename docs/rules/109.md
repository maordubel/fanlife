## 109 · FAN LIFE trivia is forged in English from each club's own records, in four levels (12.10.2026)

Owner: *"learn the Hapoel Hebrew questions… integrate everything into the one trivia feature, tagged so every club and ALL FOOTBALL use it smartly; four levels; everything in English. Always."*
1. **`lib/trivia/forge.ts` is the Question Forge**: The Worker's templates (opponent, score, season, true/false, scorer, starting three, European order, kit maker/sponsor, honours, season finish, shirt number, position, decade, era set, arrival order, timeline year) run over ANY club's `ClubData`. `withForgedTrivia` (in `loadClubBase` and the desk provider) adds it to every club. Hapoel's Hebrew Worker bank stays The Worker's `/trivia`; in FAN LIFE Hapoel plays only the forge bank.
2. **English or nothing.** A Hebrew/Greek name is rendered through `content/manual/names-en/*` (reviewed dictionaries); a name not in them is not guessed — the question is not made. `isLatin` is asserted on every prompt, option and explanation (`tests/trivia/forge.test.ts`).
3. **Four levels = names over the engine's 2–5** (`lib/trivia/levels.ts`): Easy · Medium · Hard · Expert. The run still climbs three stages; the pill shows the level name. Level comes from the event (final/derby/Europe easier, a league night decades ago harder) and from how prominent the man is in his own archive.
4. **Tags:** every forged question carries `facets` (`club:`, `template:`, `level:`, `kind:`, `decade:`, `derby`, `final`, `kit`, `honour`, `player`…). ALL FOOTBALL's per-club cap is `stratifiedCap` — 25/30/25/20% by level, rotating templates.
5. **Never quizzed:** a persecution, death, war or disaster on a timeline (`SOMBRE`) — remembered, not a question. A false true/false is false by the record (an honours list is only negated when complete).
6. Spellings are canonical per normalised key (opponent = the most frequent spelling; makers/sponsors via `brand()`), so the sanitizer drops <1%.
