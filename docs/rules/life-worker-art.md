## life-worker-art · THE WORKER's art in LIFE: Tel Aviv art for Tel Aviv, universal art for every city, Hapoel's own stays in Hapoel's pack (24.11.2026)

Owner decision, 24.11.2026: "THE WORKER has lots and lots of Tel Aviv graphics you may use." The map of what may go where is `lib/life/worker-art/catalog.json` (every file, classified after looking at contact sheets of every family; written up in `docs/life/WORKER-ART-CATALOG.md`).

1. **Four classes.** `tlv`: Tel Aviv and Israeli places, papers and sounds (Allenby, Bloomfield, the promenade, Jaffa, the bus station, Egged tickets, Toto forms). LIFE uses these for Tel Aviv and the Israeli-city clubs only. `universal`: rooms, a kitchen, a classroom, a schoolyard, props, keepsakes (the Box, the kit bag), textures, haze, grain and townsperson portraits. Every city may use these, after colour handling to its skin through the colour policy. `hapoel-only`: crests, Hapoel shirts and kits, Gate 5 and Gate 7, Ussishkin, chapter plates, transitions, and the named canon (Pogi, Kobi, Rachel, Ofir, Amit, Keren, Efi and the rest). These stay inside Hapoel's own pack and never appear on another club. `no`: never reused.
2. **Real people.** Players and other real people (Sinai, Gershon, Elimelech, Michel, the Supergol cards, the 1980/81 booklet) appear only in Hapoel's pack, and only where THE WORKER's sources already place them. Nobody's likeness is redrawn, recoloured or renamed into another club.
3. **Refused.** Files whose origin THE WORKER records as unknown (`confidence 0`: the street-figure and street-prop batch, the 1986 ticket and press pages, the unresolved FAN LIFE imports), third-party maker and sponsor marks, magazine covers, commercial or broadcast recordings, and THE WORKER's own social cards. The 1989 and 1995 films are `tlv`, but their licence is unverified, so they need the owner's yes before they go on screen.
4. **THE WORKER is the source of truth.** A LIFE copy is written only by `scripts/life/worker-art/import.ts` from a WORKER checkout, into `public/life/worker/<class>/`, under the SAME file name. A re-run overwrites it; nobody edits it by hand or saves it under a second name. To change a picture, change it in THE WORKER and re-run the import. `lib/life/worker-art/imported.json` lists what was written.
5. **Yellow.** Every copy is de-yellowed with `scripts/brand/deyellow.py` where needed, and its encoded bytes are decoded and scanned with `lib/isYellow.ts`. A lossy encode that invents yellow moves up to near-lossless or lossless (rules 8, 27). `tests/life-worker-art.test.ts` scans every copy.
6. **Hapoel's pack needs no copy.** `public/life/*` already mirrors THE WORKER byte for byte under the same paths, through the LIFE upstream import. Hapoel-only art is read from there.
