## 111 · Match highlights are watch-only, and the draft is a slot machine in a fixed order (12.10.2026)

Owner (12.10.2026): "display only, highlights only, many different scenes, the right kits, the line-ups shown properly" · "the Rumble's slot system in the manager; slots come up by themselves: GK, DF, MF, FW, DF, MF, FW, MF, DF, GK …".
1. **One theatre** (`components/highlights/Theatre.tsx`): a 256×160 canvas, procedural pixel men (`sprite.ts`), the broadcast line-up board, and the owner's celebration films cut by `scripts/legends/cut-celebrations.py` into `public/legends/fx/`. Nobody plays — the controls only skip, speed up, watch again. A reel is the server's (`lib/highlights/types.ts`); scenes are seeded (`scenes.ts`: 13 builds × 5 endings × mirror, fouls, offside), so a match always plays the same.
2. **A name tag only for a man the record names in that moment** (scorer, assist, keeper). A named man the reel cannot find runs UNNAMED; a man who came on is `bench` — named when he acts, never a stand-in, never on the board.
3. **Kits:** each man in his club's documented kit nearest his years (`kitFor`); the away side changes to its away list on a clash; keepers wear what neither side wears.
4. **Paint per page** (`lib/highlights/page.ts → highlightsAllow`, rule 95): on a club page a banned colour never shows — banned grass becomes the Rumble's night felt, a film whose colours cross the ban becomes lettering, a yellow card is "Booked". The hub passes no predicate.
5. **LEGENDS MANAGER** attaches the reel on the server (`lib/legends/kits.ts → withHighlights`); a fresh result plays by itself once. **Royal Rumble** shows it behind the `highlights` switch (Settings → Features, owner preview while off); the match centre stays one tap away.
6. **The Legends draft deals itself** (`lib/legends/draft.ts`): `DRAFT_ORDER` (the quotas exactly, FW for the optional 18th); a line that cannot show a legal trio yields to the next line still owed. There is no "choose a group" move. The screen is the Rumble cabinet (`SlotReel`, `rumble.module.css`); a tap signs (rule 24).
