## 124 · THE WEEKLY: FAN LIFE is a 1970–72 comic football weekly — one kit, one menu, no sideways scroll (13.10.2026)

Owner approved every board (Design canvas "The Weekly", 82 boards) and: *"merge everything into main, thoroughly, for every page"* · *"live for everyone at once"* · *"only The Weekly's fonts"* · *"no yellow ban — a ban on the RIVAL's colour per club"* · *"I don't like sliding or sideways-scrolling menus — never"*. Skill: `the-weekly-design`. LIFE is not part of it.
1. **Faces by job, through tokens only** (`app/magazine.css`): `--mag-brand` Shrikhand (mastheads, page and section titles) · `--mag-shout` Knewave (FIXED exclamations only — never a name or data; it lacks đ ł ș) · `--mag-display` Weekly Head (Archivo 900 italic, wdth 80: heads, names, buttons) · `--mag-mono` Weekly Caps (Archivo 800, wdth 75: labels) · `--mag-goal` Ultra (numbers, scores, years, prices) · `--mag-body` Weekly Body (Archivo variable). Greek falls back to Fira Sans (Extra Condensed) and Dela Gothic One. All self-hosted latin + latin-ext (`scripts/brand/weekly-fonts.py`) — č ć đ š ž render. **This supersedes rule 121 for FAN LIFE** (Oswald and Anton are gone); Bowlby One stays only where rule 121 kept it for the wordmark's legacy screens.
2. **Inks:** ink, paper, vermilion, navy, cyan, green, orange, a little yellow (`--mag-yellow`, defined once; at most one accent area per screen). **The rival lock** re-points the banned family at the token through `[data-rival-no]` (`rivalBans()` in `lib/clubs/theme.ts`, now incl. yellow for hue 38–70): red → the club's primary, green → navy, blue → the club's primary, yellow → orange. A rival's crest on a club page prints in one ink (`bannedHere()` → `data-rival`). Never a raw colour in a component; the rival block sits ABOVE the club-signature block in magazine.css (`tests/club-signature.test.ts` forbids colour words after it).
3. **The kit is `app/weekly.css` (`.wk-*`)**; each family has its own sheet in `app/weekly/<family>.css` with its own prefix (hub `wkh-`, corner `wkc-`, club `wkk-`, games A `wga-`, games B `wgb-`, legends `wkl-`/`lgm-`). A panel that lifts is `wk-panel wk-lift` — a `shadow-*` token in .tsx fails `tests/brand.test.ts`. master.css's legacy `.fl .muted` is neutralised for `.wk-k.muted` in the kit.
4. **No sideways scrolling, anywhere.** No `overflow-x-auto`, `snap-x`, rail, carousel, ‹ › pager or sideways tab strip: use wrapping grids, `.wk-folders`, `.wk-seg` (≤4), a `.wk-fold` (`<details>`), a `.wk-seeall` that opens in place or in a sheet. Screens are checked for scrollWidth == width at 390 and 1440.
5. **One menu:** the masthead's MENU button opens the contents (`components/weekly/Contents.tsx`, data in `lib/weekly/menu.ts` and `lib/weekly/contents.ts`): every section, all thirteen gates by wall number with the beloved gate marked, your corner, every club, FAN LIFE — a sheet on a phone, a dropped spread on a desktop, portalled with the page's rival lock and club paint, `z-[91]`, Escape closes and focus returns. The club button (`ClubSwitcher`) is a crest grid in a sheet.
6. **The GOAL! moment is global** (`components/clubs/PlayFx.tsx`): any element that appears with `data-verdict` fires it — `wrong` is the quiet navy "Wide!", anything else a win; `data-shout` names the word, `data-shout-sub` adds a line, `data-shout="off"` keeps the old ✓/✗. No sound, reduced motion stops it. A game never builds a second one.
7. **Pictures are comic plates** (`<Plate art ink>`: two layers, `public/brand/weekly/plates/<art>-{line,tone}.webp`, the tone filled with a token ink; `scripts/brand/weekly-plates.py`). Original drawings, not real players.
8. **A trivia board is keyed by its seed only when the link pinned one**: starting a run sets the run cookie and Next re-renders the page, so an unpinned seed re-minted on that render remounted the board to its start card (found 13.10.2026 on main too).
