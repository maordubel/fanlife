# Shop branding: the neck label only, a postcard in every order (owner, 15.11.2026)

- **FAN LIFE prints on the inside neck label of a garment, and nowhere else** — no sleeve mark, no back-of-neck mark. Small and dignified: the seal, FAN LIFE, a rule and the address, at 56% of the label area, centred. One ink (`public/brand/fanlife/neck-label-ink.png`); the light ink (`neck-label-light.png`) on dark colours, chosen per colour by `labelInk`. Only where the printer offers a label print and the design does not use it. A club's rival colours never appear: the label has no colour of its own.
- **Every order carries the FAN LIFE card.** Printify inserts print one side only, so the card in the box is `public/brand/fanlife/package-insert.png` (6×4 in, 1800×1200 px, 300 dpi, everything inside a 160 px safe margin; source `scripts/brand/package-insert.html`): the thank-you, the care line and a QR to the site (utm_source=postcard). Club-neutral, on cream, with the colour FAN LIFE seal (the neck label stays one ink; the card carries the colour logo — owner, 15.11.2026). The two-sided `postcard-front.png` / `postcard-back.png` stay for a printer that offers both sides. The design is saved in Printify → Store settings → Branding → Package inserts; enabling it costs $0.25 per order on the free plan ($0.15 with Premium), is not offered on Express orders. Enabled 15.11.2026; the customer carries the 25¢ — `price()` adds `INSERT_CENTS` after the margin and the sale floor keeps it.
- **Soft prints**: when a picture is smaller than the print it covers, the press sends a high-quality enlargement (Lanczos + light sharpen, up to 4×, 36 MP) and records `printify.upscaled`. It adds no detail; the desk names the products whose original should be replaced by a sharper file.
- **Two sides on objects, twice on a mug, eight different looks** (owner, 15.11.2026: "two-sided printing on a pillow… perfect mock-ups"). An object seen from both sides (cushion, tote, blanket, pennant — `twoSidedByDefault`: no sizes, not kids, prints ≥ 86% wide) prints its picture on the back too, unless the owner chose a side; wear keeps a clean back, hats are never doubled. Drinkware wraps (mug, tumbler, bottle, ≥ 1.8:1) carry the art twice, one each side of the handle. The press records `printify.side`, and the gallery follows it: one take of each scene before a second take (person-1 before person-2), the live picture first, the plain front second and, on a two-sided product, the back third.
