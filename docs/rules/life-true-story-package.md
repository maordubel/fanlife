## life-true-story-package · LIFE is based on a true story, and it plays with what the club already owns (28.11.2026)

Owner, 28.11.2026: the story really is drawn from things that happened, to him and to supporters he knows, reworked for the game; that is part of the brand, of the marketing and of how the game feels. The game is "GTA joined to a documentary, with something of The Sims: collecting, feeling, friendship". Use the kit SVGs, our cards, the graphics library and the archive wisely; brand it properly as LIFE and as FAN LIFE; nicer speech bubbles, a film's feel, better sound.

1. **"Based on a true story" is said, and only as it is true.** The stamp on the title, the line on the Coming soon page and the slate say it. The wording that may be printed: the story is drawn from real events in real supporters' lives, retold as a game; the matches are as the archive records them (rule life-story-watch still holds: a date or a score only from the Fitter). No real person is named or shown by it, and nothing personal about the owner is printed.
2. **The slate.** Each time the street is given back at a new hour (a new life, a mission taken, a new age) the documentary says where and when: the town, the part of the day and the clock, and the year the life has reached when the club's life has a birth year.
3. **A conversation is a scene.** The frame closes to a film's shape while somebody speaks; the speaker's painted face is a card beside the words (rule life-day-and-weight §7).
4. **Two marks, in their places** (rule two-logos): the edition's mark beside "A FAN LIFE game" on the title and the pause screen (`markFor(host)`: FAN LIFE's roundel; THE WORKER's badge only on Hapoel's own host), the club's own crest beside its name (`crestFor`), LIFE's wordmark as the game's own. LIFE's lime is never printed on a club whose pages may not carry green.
5. **The club's own things, chosen by the year** (`lib/clubs/life/things.ts` reads them on the server, `lib/life/hood/things.ts` chooses): the first shirt is the home shirt of the season he was seven (the kit archive's studio-cut SVG, `kitArt`), never one from the future; a sticker found in the street is one of THE ALBUM's own, a man who was at the club in the year the life has reached, then the nearest in time, the same for every player of the same life (no dice). Stickers found are kept with the life and shown in the kit bag, which opens THE ALBUM. A club with nothing on file shows nothing; nothing is invented.
6. **The street is heard** (`lib/life/hood/soundScene.ts`, from the recordings in `public/life/sfx`): the street's air by the hour, the ground's crowd on a matchday evening growing with every step toward it, his own steps, and a cue for every screen, choice, pickup and step done. Behind a menu the world is turned down, never off.
7. **The moment** (`lib/life/hood/story/moments.ts`, from the owner's screenplay: "years later somebody will remember which of them you did"). A chapter's last line may end on a gesture of the boy's own, one of two and neither the right one. It is remembered, never scored: it moves no money, bond or reputation; the aftermath card says what he did and one later chapter's card says who remembers it. One memory to a card. Leaving without doing either is a way too, and nothing is said of it. Words: `story.moment.<mission>.*`; no year, score or club in them.
8. **Coming back** (`lib/life/hood/resume.ts`): a continued life opens on one line about the last thing he kept, or about whoever is waiting, before any list.
