## life-story-watch · LIFE always has a mission in front of you, and a film is a recorded link or nothing (23.11.2026)

Owner, 23.11.2026, in meaning: one flow of game and story with freedom of choice and always a mission ahead that can go several ways; real history and emotion kept; our YouTube and real match highlights joined to the game as a viewing experience; the studio makes the transitions. Design: `docs/life/LIFE-STORY-FLOW-2026-11-23.md`.
1. **Every mission is the same loop**: a person gives it, 2–4 approaches (two always open), one complication, one converging last step, an aftermath that changes people, money, reputation and the Box. `nextMissions` (`lib/life/hood/story/director.ts`) is never empty, in any state, for any club; `tests/life-story-director.test.ts` walks seeded lives for every core club.
2. **A chapter is dated only by the Fitter.** READY over the years the chapter is lived → the archive row as approved; anything else → a personal chapter, no date, no score. Story words never hold a year, a score or a club.
3. **A YouTube id is never typed from memory, built from a title or found by search.** `lib/life/watch/data.ts` cites, for each link, the repository file that records it, and `tests/life-watch.test.ts` opens that file. Nothing recorded → `watchFor` returns null and the card shows no film.
4. **Embeds are `youtube-nocookie.com`, loaded only after a tap**, with a plain "Watch on YouTube" link and the footage credited to its uploader. Continue is always enabled: a film is never a gate.
5. **The studio is asked, not triggered.** `scripts/life/transition-requests.ts` derives the interstitials from the story graph and the Fitter into `scripts/football-stories/life/transition-requests.json`; rendering, uploading and publishing stay the studio's own steps.
