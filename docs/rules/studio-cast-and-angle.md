# The cast, the angle and the story material

Owner, 17.11.2026: "the system, the writer, the director and the editor must understand the material, read it right, vary from film to film, find the interesting and important points, understand football and competition and how tension is built — create heroes, create tragedies; every film a world of its own, with life, characters and stories." The same day: "every upgrade that gives more content, more information, more documentary, more story material".

1. **The cast is read from the record** (`studio/characters.py`).
   - **The hero** is the player whose goals carried the season, weighted by the goals that decided matches and the goals in finals and deciders. He is planted early ("Remember the name …", with his count) and paid off on his big night ("And who else but …").
   - **The wound** is the night it went wrong: a lost final, an elimination, else the worst defeat. It is said on that night.
   - A season whose archive names fewer than half of its goals' scorers has no hero. A count from incomplete scorers is said "on record".
2. **Every film has an angle**: hero, road, tragedy, redemption, siege or rise, chosen from the record. The angle sets the opening line and leads the discovery titles, so two seasons never open or sell the same. An angle line is never about the film itself (director rule 6).
3. **The dossiers' material is told, not left in the ledger**:
   - every change of manager, on its date;
   - the final's meaning and its crowd, after its result;
   - the centenary;
   - the supporters' celebration, with the joy;
   - the league finish and the club's standing, in the verdict;
   - the European road, by its ties (which the line cites).
4. **The odds before kick-off**: ClubElo ratings on the day of each archive match (`studio/elo.py`, fetched weekly in GitHub Actions). The underdog night is said before the result ("The ratings gave … about one chance in …"), and the strongest side met is named. The source is ClubElo (clubelo.com).
5. **Drive folder names in Hebrew** are read and tagged as they are; nothing is renamed in the owner's Drive.
