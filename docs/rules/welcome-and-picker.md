# The welcome, "Pick your club" and the picture contents

**Owner, 23.11.2026.** "Not good enough, not 'right' enough and not nearly beautiful enough. Far more flowing, graphic, inviting. It is still not clear what the user will experience — give an experience. An opening screen that comes up really fast while the rest of the site loads, a graphic welcome with a quick explanation. 'Pick your club' must give far more choices and be far more beautiful."

- **The welcome** (`components/home/Welcome.tsx`, prefix `.wko`): plain HTML at the top of the front page, raised before the first paint by one inline line (`WELCOME_BOOT`, `lib/home/art.ts`) on a first visit only. Three beats — pick your club, play, open the archive — then "Kick off", which lands on the picker. Skip and Escape lower it. Its pictures are CSS backgrounds under `html[data-welcome]`, so a returning supporter downloads none of them. Without scripts it never shows, so it can never trap anyone. `/?welcome=1` shows it again.
- **Pick your club** (`components/home/ClubPicker.tsx`, prefix `.wkq`): the terrace picture carries the question; every open club is a sticker in its own colours, filtered by country or by typing; a returning supporter gets his club first, as a ticket. Under the wall: three more ways in (Surprise me · All clubs, A–Z · No club yet?) and the clubs in the workshop with the vote. Phone: three stickers a row, nothing sideways (rule 124). Desktop: the picture beside a wall of four.
- **Inside this issue is pictures** (`components/home/ContentsTiles.tsx`, prefix `.wkx`): each feature is a picture card whose line says what happens behind it.
- **Pictures are the owner's** (packs vol. 02 and vol. 03 of 23.11.2026): masters in `brand/source/graphics-vol-02-2026-11-23/` and `brand/source/png-elements-vol-03-2026-11-23/`, cut by `scripts/brand/art-vol2.py` to `public/brand/fanlife/art/` (scenes) and `public/brand/fanlife/bits/` (cut-outs). Take them through `scene()` and `bit()`.
- All of it is the new front page: behind the switch `frontDoors`, owner preview on.
- **Lesson (a broken screen in QA):** `.wkw`, `.wkc` and `.wkt` were already taken (the loading cover, the club page, the watch strip) and the loading cover's rule showed the welcome on every visit. Before naming a prefix: `grep -rhoE "\.<prefix>\b" app components --include=*.css`.
- **The shop window** (owner, 23.11.2026, the same morning: "the shop is missing — sales are something we will have to push wisely"): `components/shop/ShopWindow.tsx` (prefix `.wkz`) is a spread of its own on the front page between the clubs and the contents — the picture, one promise, three reasons to trust the order, four products (a sale first, then what sells, one per design), one way in. The welcome gets a fourth beat, "Wear it". Both appear ONLY while the shop is open and has products: a door never leads to an empty room. A line on the window is a promise the shop keeps today (printed to order, Europe first: rule shop-europe-first).
- **The front page comes up at once** (owner, 23.11.2026: "the whole point of the opening screen is that it opens fast while the rest loads behind it; if there is loading the user must understand it; the wait as hidden as possible"). `app/page.tsx` is two streams: `WelcomeDoor` waits only for its switch and the shop's open/closed (both cached), `Home` waits for the clubs, feeds and shop. Until `Home` arrives a loading cover (`.wkfl`, the house `PressWait`) is on the page — held back 0.35 s so a fast page never flashes it — and the welcome over it says "The paper is on its way". The welcome's buttons are answered by the boot line itself, so Kick off works before any bundle has loaded; it lands on the picker as soon as the picker exists. Never put a slow read in front of the welcome: anything the welcome needs must be cached or cheap.
- **Measured on production, 23.11.2026** (the owner's browser, Athens): an anonymous visitor gets the front page from the edge copy — headers in 39–49 ms, the whole 159 KB page in 67–82 ms, welcome included. What the eye then waits for is the styles (10 files, 122 KB, up to 0.8 s) and the welcome's picture (277 KB on a desktop, started 0.9 s after the styles). So: the picture is preloaded with the styles, a 0.2 KB copy of it is inside the stylesheet so it is there from the first frame, the desktop cut is 185 KB, and the welcome does not wait for the shop file (it held it a full second on a cold instance). A page that is not in the edge copy yet took about 1 s (`/shop`, a club's home) and `/clubs` got the edge's boot screen at 1.2 s.
