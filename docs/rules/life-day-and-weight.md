## life-day-and-weight · LIFE's street has one clock, and a frame has a budget (28.11.2026)

Owner, 28.11.2026: the new LIFE replaces the old one; day and night are different and the lighting is in order; the game was "very heavy" and must run and flow, on a phone and on a desk, each in its own right.

1. **The club's LIFE address is the new game.** `/clubs/<id>/life` opens the open neighbourhood (`components/clubs/life/hood`, through `HoodDoor`, fetched after the page paints). The room engine it replaces is `?engine=classic`, for the owner only. LIFE stays closed to the public (rule life-closed); the owner's door is `/master/life`.
2. **One clock, set by the story.** `lib/life/hood/daylight.ts` is the day: `lightAt(minutes)` blends the hours (night, first light, cool morning, noon, the gold afternoon, sunset, the approved dusk, the last blue, night). Taking a mission moves the clock to the hour it happens at (`missionStart`: school in the morning, matchday in the gold hour so the walk to the ground ends under floodlights); after that it only runs forward, one game minute per 1.5 s of play, never in a menu, a card or a conversation. The clock is saved with the life.
3. **The approved dusk is a keyframe, number for number** (`DUSK_LIGHT`, `tests/life-hood-day.test.ts`). A new hour is a new key in that file, never a tint over the picture. Warm light is orange-amber, never yellow (rule 8); the test scans every minute.
4. **What is ON follows the hour, by what a thing is, not by a list.** `look/daylight.ts` finds additive light (it fades to nothing by day), things lit from inside (they go back to dark glass or a pale panel), emissive paint, the road (it dries) and walls painted at dusk (`userData.dayWall`: lifted by day). What the player steers by is `userData.dayFixed` and never dims.
5. **A frame's budget.** Measured 28.11.2026 on the street's first view, phone quality: 3,720,000 triangles before, 430,000 after. Three rules made that and keep it:
   - **An instance scaled to nothing still costs all of its triangles.** Instanced people are packed (`im.count` = who is drawn); nobody is hidden by a zero matrix.
   - **Only who can be seen is drawn.** Past the middle distance a supporter is the far body (about a hundred triangles); behind the lens and past the haze he is not drawn; small things leave the camera's layer past the distance they read at, and stay out of the wet road's mirror (`look/detail.ts`, layers, never `visible`).
   - **What is not seen is not drawn.** Behind a full panel the street is one still frame; the title's crane is drawn every other frame.
6. **The governor measures, it does not guess.** `stage.ts` keeps a moving average of real frame times and steps the drawing scale down (and the mirror's redraw rate) before the game slows, and back up when there is room. `stage.stats()` and `stage.profile()` are what a claim about weight is made from; `/qa/life-world` exposes them as `__hoodGl()` and `__hoodProfile()`.
7. **The key people are painted once, per part of the world** (`life-cast-<people>-<who>`, rule life-flow-art): invented faces, never a real person, never a club's colour. A conversation shows the speaker's portrait as a card when it has been painted, THE WORKER's universal face until then.
