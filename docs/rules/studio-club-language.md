# Studio: the club's own language on screen (owner, 16.11.2026)

Owner, 16.11.2026: *"From now on, when you edit in the studio, also add text in the club's language, such as Greek —
it attracts viewers."*

- Every Football Stories format (DOCU, Shorts, Reels, CLASSIFIED, thumbnails) carries on-screen text in the club's own
  language beside the English: the chapter cards, the question and turn cards, the title card and the thumbnail kicker.
  Greek for the Greek clubs, Hebrew for the Israeli clubs, Arabic for Wydad and Raja, and so on.
- The narration and the subtitles stay English. The second language is a smaller line under the English line, in the
  same card, never a card of its own.
- The language and the font come from the club pack (`world.json` → `language`). The font must carry the script
  (Greek, Hebrew, Arabic, the Yugoslav diacritics).
- Only words that are translated faithfully from the English line, or the club's own name as the club writes it.
  Nothing is invented in the second language that the English does not say.
