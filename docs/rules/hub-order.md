# The hub and a club's home are put in order, not redesigned

**Owner, 12.11.2026.** "I did not ask to change the graphics. I asked for order: easy and comfortable to use, everything clear and reachable, so a visitor feels at ease, misses no feature and gets full, flowing exposure. Do not replace the design — put the design in order." The same day he rejected a fold that hid sections behind a button ("terrible") and asked for the clubs as a slide and for the "next match" box to go.

- **Nothing is hidden and nothing is restyled.** Every block keeps its own look. On a phone the order is set in CSS (`order`), the desktop spread keeps its grid.
- **The hub's order on a phone:** masthead → your club (continue / pick) → the clubs, as a slide → "Inside this issue" → Legends Manager → LIFE → the counter (shirt market + shop) → Football Stories → the cover (XI, quiz) → your pins, the dock, the ballot, the editor's letter, the back pages.
- **"Inside this issue" is the index of our own features** — clubs, Legends Manager, LIFE, the shop, the market, the films — one cell each, in the page's order. A new feature of ours gets a cell there and a block on the page; that is how none is missed.
- **The clubs are a slide on a phone** (the same sticker cards on one row, the next one peeking). This is the owner's decision for this row and replaces "never a rail" here only; menus and tabs still never scroll sideways.
- **No "next match" box on the hub.** The stop-press strip still carries the fixture line.
- **A club's home on a phone:** masthead → today's pick and Kick off → Pick a gate → the club's Legends → LIFE → the shop's shelf → the film → the next match → your card → "Did you know?" → the contents rows.
- A change to either page is a change of order or of content — a new look needs the owner's yes first, shown to him before code.
