# LIFE — the visual floor (23.11.2026)

Seven reference frames supplied for LIFE. They are the MINIMUM the game is judged against, for look and for play feel.

What they fix:
- **Light**: dusk or night, floodlights over the roofs, warm street lamps, wet ground that reflects, haze at the end of the street, bloom on lights and flares.
- **Density**: a street full of supporters in the club's colours, parked and moving cars, stalls with lit awnings, bunting and string lights, the ground visible ahead.
- **Camera**: third person, the supporter small in the lower third (about a fifth of the screen height), the street and the ground filling the rest.
- **People**: stylised, warm, expressive faces; a mate who gestures while he talks; stewards and police as readable silhouettes.
- **HUD**: rounded glass panels, a round minimap with a ring, one objective line with distance, a kick-off clock, two round thumb buttons (talk, sprint/sneak), subtitles low on the picture, a ground arrow for the next step.
- **Screens**: a title screen ("The city is your terrace."), a district map with waypoints, a kit bag.

Frames are references for direction only: nothing in them is a fact about a club, and each club's street carries its own colours, city and ground under its rival-colour policy.
