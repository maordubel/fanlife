# Route parity — pass D, 2013–2026 (28.9.2026)

`IMPLEMENTATION-PASS-PROGRAMMER-2026-09-27` §3: the same life through thirteen routes, and for
each route `chapter | meaningful actions | unique screen | unique consequence | callback written |
minutes active`. **Failure condition:** a route that repeatedly receives "same scene + two
different lines" while another route receives mechanics and locations.

How this was measured: the headless world (`tests/fixtures/lifeWorldSim.ts`, the real engine and
dialogue runner over the rooms of `scenes.ts`) walked in `tests/life-d-pass-2026-09-28.test.ts`
and the existing 90-E suites, and the browser probe `scripts/life/pass-d-probe.mjs` for the
chapters this pass rebuilt. "Minutes active" is game minutes the player's own verbs cost
(`time` effects, chores, rides) on the golden path of that route — not wall-clock.

Legend: **M** = a mechanic or a place of its own (hotspots, chore, ride, a room); **L** = lines
inside a shared scene; — = the route does not touch the chapter.

## Gaps (open, named — not hidden)

1. **2023-quiet · Z05 "where were you"** — the abroad life now skips the menu (natural state:
   he lives in the other place; the evening arrives on a delayed stream). Every other life still
   chooses among four places, which is a real choice (who he stands with), not a known state.
2. **2025-eurocup · Z06** — closed this pass: the night of the final now collides with the morning
   after (`z-collide`) — the child's tournament, the partner's parents, or a meeting at eight —
   kept, moved with their yes, or lost to the night (`life:eurocup:night`).
3. **2021-promises, no-child life** — closed this pass: the kept or repaired promise now
   *happens* — sunset on the promenade with the partner or with Keren, the phone in the pocket
   during a match: face down, one honest peek, or leaving for the half (`life:promise2021`).
4. **2026-finale walk** — every route gets exactly one stop's worth of lines (by design: the walk
   is about Kobi, not the route). This is parity *by construction*, and it is the one place the
   "same scene + different lines" shape is intended.
