# Original artwork

`stadium-original.png` was generated for this kit on 25 October 2026 using the built-in image-generation tool. It is an original fictional empty football ground. No club crests, player photographs or real people's faces were supplied or inserted.

The background is embedded in T1 and T8. Their duotone treatment is part of the editable vector template. No image API is needed to reuse the templates.

## Generation prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create ONE original high resolution portrait 4:5 background photograph for the DUBEL TEAM social templates. A 1970s football matchday programme printed on cheap paper crossed with a modern sports app. View from behind an EMPTY old concrete football terrace toward the pitch, steel railings, one old floodlight tower, distant stand roof, cinematic overcast evening sky. No people at all, no faces, no club crests, no brand names, no text, no letters, no scoreboard numbers. Grainy analog black and white documentary photograph, exactly two tonal families: dark ink #121412 and off-white paper #F2EDE3. Coarse newspaper halftone dots, subtle cheap newsprint texture, slight 3px ink misregistration, distressed photocopy tonal treatment, powerful and atmospheric yet clearly legible as an empty stadium. Flat editorial photograph, NOT illustration, NOT 3D, no glossy surface, no gradients or color, no border or mockup environment. Keep useful details spread across the frame so it can serve as a crop-able photo slot in multiple template sizes. Return one finished background image.

## Other assets

The football seal, hourglass, phone frame, shirt placeholder, pitch diagrams, comic panel and six highlight icons are original vector artwork created in `source/design.mjs`.

The supplied Anton and Courier Prime assets come from the existing Fan Life project font collection. Archivo Regular is instantiated from the official Google Fonts Archivo variable font at weight 400 and width 100. The included font license files describe permitted reuse.
