# The press kit — the collage look (owner, 23.11.2026)

Seven pieces made in the owner's Google Flow (Nano Banana, 23.11.2026) from his own brief: fictional archival newspaper
photographs of anonymous people, reproduced as aged halftone on warm newsprint, and one blank newsprint sheet.
No real person, club, badge, number or lettering is in any of them, so every club's film may use them.

| file | what | how it is used |
|---|---|---|
| paper.webp | blank aged newsprint | the ground of every press scene and of the front page |
| crowd.webp | a packed terrace, full frame | a duotone under the club's colour |
| ground.webp | a leather ball on the pitch, a floodlight pylon, a stand | the quiet scene |
| faithful.webp | five supporters from behind, scarves up | a pasted photograph |
| striker.webp · keeper.webp · celebrate.webp | cut-outs with a paper outline (cut by `/tmp`-free code: see docu/press.py notes) | the figures pasted on a scene |

A cut-out stands for "a footballer", never for a named player (docs/rules/studio-pictures.md §2): no caption, card or
name is ever attached to one.
