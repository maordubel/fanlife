# Football Fairy Tales — identity (phase 0, 11.11.2026)

**The Paper Theatre.** Every episode is a real handmade mechanical pop-up book, photographed on a wooden desk in
morning window light. Nothing is drawn flat: the clubs, the grounds and the heroes are cut card, kraft paper and torn
coloured paper, and every hero is a paper puppet held together by **brass split pins**.

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| World | A photographed pop-up book. Real paper fibres, cut edges, pull-tabs, soft layered shadows, shallow depth of field. |
| Signature | The **brass split pin** — the dot of the wordmark, the joint of every puppet, the pivot of transitions. |
| Base palette | kraft `#C8A774` · cream card `#F1E6CF` · ink `#2A1E14` · brass `#C49E4A` · the desk. |
| Club plates | The club's own two colours are the only printed colours in an episode; the rival's colours never appear (club pack `bannedRivalHues`). Crests are printed onto card, multiplied into the paper. |
| Type | Fraunces — Black, SOFT 100, WONK 1 for the title letters (each letter a separate card); Bold, tracked caps for FOOTBALL. |
| Motion | Stop-motion: overlays and confetti on twos (12 fps), poses held on threes, a slow camera push, a small frame-to-frame boil. |
| Transition | The page turn — the only one the series uses. |
| Sound | Paper, card, brass: page turns, card pops, a pin's tick; the narrator young and bright, the music under him. |
| Audience | General audience (owner, 11.11.2026): grown-up fans who watch with their children. No toddler language, no lullabies. |

Flow prompt stem for every plate: *"Tabletop photograph of a handmade mechanical pop-up book … warm morning window
light from the left … real paper texture, visible fibres, hand-cut edges, pull-tabs, brass split pins, soft layered
shadows, shallow depth of field, miniature stop-motion feel. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos."* Text and
crests are always added by the compositor (`papertheatre.py`), never generated.
