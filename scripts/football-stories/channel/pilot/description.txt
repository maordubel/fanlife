In 1999–2000, Hapoel Tel Aviv had not won the Israeli league for twelve seasons. Then came five days in May: the title secured at Bnei Yehuda on 13 May, and on 17 May a State Cup final against Beitar Jerusalem that went to penalties.

This film follows the whole season from the terraces, in order: the UEFA Cup trip to Yerevan and the defeats to Celtic, the autumn run, the derbies with Maccabi Tel Aviv, the February slump that made supporters fear the worst, the cup run through Acre, Maccabi Tel Aviv, Be'er Sheva and Maccabi Haifa, and the double.

Every score, date and table on screen is printed from FanLife's archive records and the sources listed below. Where the archive does not name every scorer, the film does not name any.
