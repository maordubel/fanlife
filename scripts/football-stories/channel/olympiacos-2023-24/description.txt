Olympiacos were three goals down at home to Maccabi Tel Aviv in March 2024. Twelve weeks later they won the Europa Conference League final in Athens — the first European trophy ever won by a Greek club. This is the road in between, told from the stands.

On 29 May 2024, Olympiacos beat Fiorentina 1–0 after extra time in the Europa Conference League final — the first European trophy ever won by a Greek club, and it was won in Athens, at the home of AEK. Ayoub El Kaabi headed the winner in the 116th minute.

This film follows the whole road from the stands, in order: Genk and Čukarički in August, a Europa League group that ended in third place, three managers in one season, José Luis Mendilibar's arrival in February, the 1–4 home defeat to Maccabi Tel Aviv and the 6–1 comeback in Bačka Topola in front of 370 people, three penalties saved by Konstantinos Tzolakis in Istanbul, El Kaabi's hat-trick at Villa Park, and the final.
