In 2002–03, Celtic were knocked out of the Champions League in August and ended the season with nothing on paper: a UEFA Cup final lost to FC Porto after extra time in Seville on 21 May, and a league title lost to Rangers by one goal of goal difference four days later.

This film follows the whole road from the stands, in order: Basel in August, Sūduva, Blackburn Rovers, Celta Vigo, Stuttgart, the ten days in March around Liverpool, the semi-final against Boavista, and the journey of an estimated 80,000 supporters to Seville — the support that won FIFA's Fair Play Award that year.

Every score, date and table on screen is printed from FanLife's archive records (UEFA match records) and the sources listed below. The crowd in Seville is an estimate in every source and the film says so.
